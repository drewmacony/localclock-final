    Backup

    Keep the system clock synchronized

    Windows: Settings → Time & language → Date & time → turn on “Set time automatically” and “Set time zone automatically”, then “Sync now”.

    macOS: System Settings → General → Date & Time → turn on “Set time and date automatically” and “Set time zone automatically using your current location”.
